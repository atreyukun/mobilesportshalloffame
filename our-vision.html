A Shared Vision

One city. One legacy. One future.

A Shared Vision is the guiding principle of the Mobile Sports Hall of Fame: transforming Mobile into a premier sports and entertainment destination by connecting our athletic history with youth development, community health, and economic opportunity.

By establishing the Legacy of Legends Museum, partnering with schools and service organizations, and inviting professional athletes native to Mobile to give back, the Hall of Fame brings together Hall of Famers, civic leaders, and citizens around a common goal.

Sports is an economic engine for the city—with the potential to bring tournaments, exhibition games, and tourist dollars—while also teaching the next generation skills that last far beyond the field.
