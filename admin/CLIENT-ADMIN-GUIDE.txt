WHY THIS EXISTS
---------------
You can update news, the featured event, partners, sponsors, and the board
roster without waiting on a developer for every change.

The website is made of simple files. When you click Save, your updates are
published to those files so the live pages can refresh—usually within about
a minute.


WHY THERE IS ALSO A PUBLISH KEY
-------------------------------
Saving needs a publish key in addition to the admin password. That key is
NOT the same as the password.

You paste it once per browser session when you Save or upload an image. It
stays in your browser only and is never stored on the public website.

Treat the publish key like a house key—do not post it publicly or share it
outside your team.


HOW TO MAKE A CHANGE
--------------------
1. Open the content admin URL and sign in with the password above.
2. Use the tabs: News, Featured event, Partners, Sponsors, Board.
3. Edit an item, then click Apply (when shown).
4. Click Save. Paste the publish key if asked.
5. Wait about one minute, then refresh the public site to confirm.


IMAGES AND LOGOS
----------------
Use Browse… next to an image or logo field to upload a file. The upload
happens right away. Then click Apply and Save so the page points to the
new file.
