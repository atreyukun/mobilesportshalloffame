Mobile Sports Hall of Fame — Site Update
========================================

Prepared for: Mobile Sports Hall of Fame stakeholders
Purpose: Progress update on the new website


OPENING
-------
A modern, mobile-friendly website for the Mobile Sports Hall of Fame is
ready for your review. It presents the Hall’s story, inductees, news, and
events in a cleaner layout, and includes a simple content admin so your
team can update many pages without waiting on a developer for every change.


WHERE TO PREVIEW
----------------
  https://atreyukun.github.io/mobilesportshalloffame/

Note: mobilesportshalloffame.net remains the current public WordPress site
until you decide to switch over officially.


WHAT’S ON THE SITE
------------------
The new site includes:

  • Home — brand-forward landing with Hall of Fame legend highlights
  • Hall of Famers — searchable inductee directory
  • Our Mission — plus Remembering the Past, Honoring the Present,
    Welcoming the Future, and Board Members
  • A Shared Vision — plus Partners and Sponsors
  • News & Events — headlines, induction-class stories, and event listings
  • Tickets — information for the museum / induction timeline
  • Contact
  • Privacy Policy and Refund Policy

Branding throughout uses the Hall’s logo and crest, with dedicated hero
imagery on each major page. A discreet Admin link appears in the footer of
every public page so staff can find the content tools quickly. From admin,
a “View site” link returns to the public homepage.


HALL OF FAMERS DIRECTORY
------------------------
The Hall of Famers section is a full directory of roughly 144 inductees.

Visitors can:
  • Search by name, sport, year, or keyword
  • Filter by letter (A–Z)
  • Filter by sport, including a Coaches filter
  • Open any card to read the full bio in a popup

Where photos are available, they appear on the cards. Many entries also
include short video clips that play inside the bio popup (for example,
Derrick Burroughs, Derrick Coleman, the 1947 Mobile Bears Baseball Team,
and other earlier inductees).

Some inductees now use two photos: one on the directory card and a second
“interior” image in the bio popup. Examples include James Taylor,
Kelli Ogdan Hillier, and Lauretta Freeman-Horn. Card photo framing has
also been improved so faces and action shots sit more naturally in the
layout.

NEWS & EVENTS
-------------
News stories now stay on this site. Visitors see a short teaser and can
use Read more to expand the full article on the page—without being sent
to the old WordPress site for those pieces.

Events are organized into current listings and a past-events archive, with
featured items highlighted on Home and on News & Events.

The Tickets page carries messaging for the Spring 2027 museum opening /
induction timeline. Former Eventbrite ticket links have been removed so
the site points to your current ticket information page instead.


CONTENT ADMIN (FOR YOUR TEAM)
-----------------------------
A password-protected content admin is available at:

  https://atreyukun.github.io/mobilesportshalloffame/admin/

Your team can update:
  • News
  • Events
  • Partners
  • Sponsors
  • Board roster

Saving uses the admin password plus a publish key (sent separately). After
Save, wait about a minute, then hard-refresh the public page to confirm.

Hall of Famers photos, bios, and videos are still updated by the build
team for now (they are not yet editable in the admin).

A separate how-to guide—CLIENT-ADMIN-GUIDE.txt—covers sign-in steps and
day-to-day editing. Login credentials are provided separately and are not
listed in this update.


WHAT WE’D LIKE FROM YOU
-----------------------
We welcome your feedback before official handoff, including:

  • Design or wording notes on any page
  • Missing or updated bios and photos
  • Corrections to names, years, sports tags, or event details
  • Confirmation of when you want this site to become the public home
    (replacing the current WordPress site)

Thank you for the materials and guidance so far. We’re ready to keep
refining as you review.
