Board Members

The people behind the mission

The Mobile Sports Hall of Fame is guided by a volunteer board of community leaders, former athletes, coaches, and business people who share one goal: perpetuating our city’s athletic heritage to make Mobile a better place to live, work and play.

The board oversees the annual induction, the Legacy of Legends Museum, and the youth programs that connect Mobile’s sporting past to its next generation.
